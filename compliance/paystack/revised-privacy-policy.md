## 2. Data we collect

We may collect:

- account and identity data, including name, email, phone, date of birth,
  password credentials managed by our authentication provider, and verification
  status;
- vendor and courier onboarding data, including business and operating address,
  category, contact details, vehicle type, NIN, guarantor details, licences,
  identity or business documents where requested, and review records;
- bank and settlement data, including bank, account number, resolved account
  name, Paystack subaccount code, and transfer-recipient code;
- order and payment data, including cart, item, service, amount, Paystack
  reference, payment status, fee breakdown, refund or dispute data, and order
  history; we do not store full payment-card details;
- fulfilment data, including pickup/delivery address, approximate or precise
  coordinates supplied for routing, courier assignment, delivery status, and
  completion time;
- communications and content, including support messages, reviews, ratings,
  photographs, listings, and dispute evidence; and
- technical data, including IP address, device/browser data, logs, cookies,
  session information, security events, and usage information.

Some data is obtained from Paystack, mapping/geocoding providers, messaging
providers, vendors, couriers, customers, and any identity-verification provider
we identify at the point of collection.

## 3. Why and on what basis we process data

We process data to create and secure accounts; verify contact, identity,
business, bank, and eligibility information; publish listings; calculate
delivery; process and reconcile payments; settle vendors and couriers; fulfil
orders; provide support; investigate fraud, complaints, refunds, and disputes;
enforce our agreements; improve the Platform; and meet legal obligations.

Depending on the activity, our lawful basis under the Nigeria Data Protection
Act 2023 may be performance of a contract, compliance with law, consent, or our
legitimate interests in operating a safe and effective marketplace after
balancing users' rights. Where sensitive personal data is processed, we rely on
an applicable additional legal condition and apply heightened safeguards.

## 4. Sharing

We share only data reasonably necessary with:

- vendors, customers, and couriers involved in an order;
- Paystack and relevant banks for payment, account resolution, settlement,
  refund, fraud, and dispute handling;
- Supabase and other hosting, authentication, storage, email, SMS, mapping,
  support, analytics, security, and professional service providers;
- identity or business-verification providers, if and when engaged and
  disclosed;
- regulators, courts, law-enforcement agencies, advisers, insurers, or other
  persons where required or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights,
  prevent harm, or comply with law; and
- a successor in a merger, financing, reorganisation, or sale, subject to
  appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal data for third-party direct marketing.

## 5. Retention

We retain data only for as long as needed for the stated purpose and applicable
legal, accounting, tax, anti-fraud, dispute, and regulatory requirements. We
will adopt and maintain a documented retention schedule. [CONFIRM SPECIFIC
PERIODS WITH COUNSEL AND ACCOUNTING REQUIREMENTS.] Expired OTPs are short-lived
operational records; transaction and compliance records may require longer
retention. Data is deleted, anonymised, or securely archived when no longer
required.

## 6. Security

Measures include encryption in transit, authenticated sessions, role-based
access, database row-level controls, restricted administrative functions,
signed payment-webhook verification, rate limits, single-use OTPs, and access
logging where available. No system is completely secure. Users must protect
their credentials and promptly report suspected compromise.

## 7. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where required, we use a
lawful transfer mechanism and assess whether recipients provide appropriate
protection under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

## 8. Cookies and device storage

We use essential cookies and browser storage for authentication, security,
cart, wishlist, and preferences. Any non-essential analytics or marketing
technology will be described and, where required, used only with consent.

## 9. Rights

Subject to applicable law, a person may request access, correction, deletion,
restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and portability, and may object
to direct marketing. A person may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection
Commission. We may verify identity before acting and may retain data where law
requires or permits it.
