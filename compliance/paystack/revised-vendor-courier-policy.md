# Green Pack Delight Vendor and Courier Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

This revised draft supplements Green Pack Delight's Terms, Acceptable Use,
Privacy, Shipping and Fulfilment, and Returns and Refunds policies.

## 1. Independent marketplace participants

Vendors and couriers operate as independent businesses or contractors and are
not Green Pack Delight employees. They control their lawful business methods,
staff, equipment, taxes, insurance, and licences, subject to marketplace rules
needed for customer protection, payment compliance, and service coordination.

Green Pack Delight does not manufacture vendor goods, perform vendor services,
hold vendor inventory as a fulfilment centre, or employ couriers.

## 2. Eligibility and verification

Partners must be at least 18, legally capable of contracting, and authorised to
conduct the relevant activity in Nigeria. All information must be accurate and
kept current.

Vendor checks may include verified email and phone; full name and date of birth;
government identity; business ownership or CAC information; operating address;
site or video inspection; sector permits; sanctions, fraud, or adverse-history
screening where lawful; and bank-account resolution through Paystack.

Courier checks may include verified contact details; full name and date of
birth; NIN and government identity; guarantor details; driving/rider licence;
vehicle ownership or authority, registration, roadworthiness and insurance;
operating area; and bank-account resolution.

**Only checks actually completed and recorded may be described as verified.**
Green Pack Delight may request updated evidence, re-verify a partner, reject an
application, restrict payment capability, or suspend a partner where evidence
is missing, inconsistent, expired, altered, or cannot be validated.

## 4. Listings and performance

Vendors must publish accurate descriptions, total prices, availability,
fulfilment conditions, safety information, and material return restrictions.
They must supply only lawful, genuine goods and competent services, honour
accepted orders, preserve order evidence, and communicate delays promptly.

Couriers must accept only jobs they can safely complete; handle items with
reasonable care; follow lawful routes and traffic requirements; maintain
truthful real-time status; protect personal data; and report loss, damage,
accident, failed delivery, or suspected prohibited items immediately.

Partners must not redirect customers to off-platform payment, manipulate
reviews or orders, misuse customer information, discriminate, harass, submit
false evidence, or engage in conduct prohibited by the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 5. Payments

Before receiving marketplace payments, a vendor must register an eligible bank
account and obtain a Paystack subaccount. Green Pack Delight currently charges
a 3% platform fee on the vendor subtotal, and Paystack's split-payment flow
allocates the remaining 97% to the vendor subaccount. Any change will be
disclosed before taking effect for new transactions.

Delivery fees are separate from the vendor subtotal. They are retained on the
platform's Paystack account during fulfilment and transferred to the approved
courier's registered bank recipient after delivery completion. Partners do not
receive an internal Green Pack wallet and cannot transfer a balance to another
user.

Paystack or the relevant bank controls settlement and transfer processing
times. Payment may be delayed, reversed, refunded, or restricted for fraud,
duplicate payment, cancellation, dispute, chargeback, legal requirement, or
policy breach. Green Pack Delight will maintain transaction and decision
records and will not withhold undisputed funds arbitrarily.

## 6. Cancellations, refunds, and disputes

Partners must comply with the published Cancellation, Returns and Refunds
Policy and cooperate by providing order, communication, preparation, pickup,
delivery, inspection, and service evidence. Green Pack Delight may facilitate
replacement, redelivery, re-performance, partial or full refund, or another
appropriate resolution. This process is marketplace administration and does
not remove any legal right.

## 7. Privacy and confidentiality

Order data may be used only to perform the relevant transaction, meet legal
obligations, or resolve a genuine complaint. Partners must protect it, restrict
staff access, avoid unsolicited marketing, and delete or securely retain it in
accordance with law and Green Pack Delight's instructions. Suspected data loss
or unauthorised disclosure must be reported immediately.

## 8. Safety, insurance, and legal compliance

Partners are responsible for licences, permits, professional standards,
product safety, food hygiene, consumer protection, employment matters, taxes,
vehicle compliance, and insurance applicable to their activity. Green Pack
Delight may require evidence at any time and may immediately restrict activity
that presents a credible danger or legal risk.

## 9. Monitoring and enforcement

Green Pack Delight may monitor objective indicators such as cancellation,
acceptance, on-time completion, complaints, refund rates, delivery records,
fraud signals, and customer ratings. Depending on severity and history, action
may include guidance, correction, listing removal, re-verification, warning,
payment review, temporary restriction, badge removal, suspension, or
termination. Serious fraud, safety, or legal risk may require immediate action
without prior notice.

Partners may contact support@greenpackdelight.com to provide evidence or request a
review. Termination does not affect accrued payment, refund, confidentiality,
tax, investigation, or legal obligations.
