# Green Pack Delight Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

This policy applies to every customer, vendor, courier, visitor, and other user
of the Green Pack Delight website, applications, marketplace, payment,
communication, and delivery services (the "Platform"). It forms part of our
Terms of Service and Vendor & Courier Policy.

## 1. General standard

Users must use the Platform lawfully, honestly, safely, and only for legitimate
marketplace activity. Users must provide accurate information, keep account
credentials secure, and cooperate with identity, business, payment, fraud, and
safety checks.

## 2. Prohibited goods and services

The following may not be listed, sold, requested, transported, promoted, or
paid for through the Platform:

- goods or services prohibited by Nigerian law or applicable regulation;
- stolen, counterfeit, pirated, illegally imported, or unlawfully obtained
  goods;
- weapons, ammunition, explosives, fireworks, or materials intended to cause
  harm;
- illegal drugs, controlled substances, drug paraphernalia, or prescription
  medicines supplied without required authorisation;
- human remains, organs, body parts, or exploitative human services;
- pornography, sexual services, or content involving minors;
- gambling, betting, pyramid, Ponzi, money-doubling, or get-rich-quick schemes;
- unlicensed financial, investment, lending, insurance, foreign-exchange,
  crypto-asset, money-transfer, or stored-value services;
- cash, cash equivalents, gift-card resale, negotiable instruments, or
  transactions intended primarily to move or disguise funds;
- hazardous, toxic, radioactive, infectious, recalled, expired, or unsafe
  products;
- protected wildlife, illegally sourced plants, or products that violate
  environmental or conservation laws;
- alcohol, tobacco, nicotine, medical, cosmetic, food, or other regulated
  products where the vendor lacks the required licence or where sale or
  delivery would violate age restrictions;
- personal data, identity documents, account credentials, hacking tools,
  malware, or services intended to compromise privacy or security;
- goods or services that infringe copyright, trademark, patent, privacy,
  publicity, or other third-party rights; and
- any item Paystack or another payment or service provider prohibits.

Green Pack Delight may prohibit additional categories based on safety,
regulatory, payment-network, or reputational risk.

## 3. Prohibited conduct

Users must not:

- provide false identity, age, address, business, licence, bank, tax, or KYC
  information, or submit altered or stolen documents;
- impersonate another person or business or create duplicate accounts to avoid
  restrictions;
- use the Platform for fraud, money laundering, terrorist financing, bribery,
  sanctions evasion, tax evasion, chargeback abuse, or transaction laundering;
- process a payment unrelated to a genuine order listed and fulfilled through
  the Platform;
- manipulate prices, orders, reviews, delivery records, referrals, search
  results, ratings, or platform fees;
- request or encourage off-platform payment to avoid controls or fees;
- harass, threaten, exploit, discriminate against, or endanger another person;
- disclose customer information except as necessary to fulfil the relevant
  order, or use it for unsolicited marketing;
- interfere with the Platform, introduce malicious code, scrape without
  permission, bypass access controls, or probe for vulnerabilities;
- open, tamper with, substitute, consume, or unlawfully retain goods entrusted
  for delivery; or
- use the Platform in a way that could expose Green Pack Delight, its users,
  Paystack, or another provider to legal, financial, or security risk.

## 4. Vendor obligations

Vendors must hold all registrations, licences, permits, insurance, and
professional qualifications required for their activity. Listings must be
accurate and must disclose material conditions, prices, availability, safety
information, and return restrictions. Vendors must not list goods they do not
possess or services they are not qualified and equipped to perform.

## 5. Courier obligations

Couriers must be approved, use a lawful and roadworthy means of transport,
follow traffic and safety laws, protect goods in transit, and update delivery
status truthfully. A courier must report loss, damage, accident, failed
delivery, suspected illegality, or a safety concern promptly.

## 6. Monitoring and enforcement

Green Pack Delight may review listings, orders, messages, verification records,
complaints, and transaction indicators where lawful and necessary. We may
remove content, reject a transaction, request further information, limit a
feature, pause a payout, suspend or terminate an account, notify Paystack, or
report conduct to a regulator or law-enforcement authority.

Where practical and legally permitted, a user may contact support to challenge
an enforcement decision and provide additional evidence. Immediate action may
be taken without notice where there is a credible fraud, safety, legal, or
security risk.

## 7. Reporting

Report suspected prohibited activity at support@greenpackdelight.com or through
www.greenpackdelight.com/help. Include the order or listing reference and any
relevant evidence. Do not place yourself at risk to collect evidence.
