# Green Pack Delight Shipping and Fulfilment Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

## 1. Marketplace model

Green Pack Delight is not a warehouse or fulfilment centre. Independent vendors
hold and prepare their own goods and perform their own services. Green Pack
Delight provides marketplace, payment, order-coordination, and delivery-
coordination technology. Delivery may be performed by an approved independent
courier or, where expressly shown, by the vendor.

## 2. Delivery and service modes

An order may use one of the following modes:

- **Direct product delivery:** the courier collects the prepared order from
  the vendor and delivers it to the customer's confirmed address.
- **Pickup and return service:** the courier collects the customer's item,
  takes it to the vendor for the service, and returns it when completed.
- **In-home service:** the vendor travels to the customer's address; no product
  shipment may be required.
- **In-store service or collection:** the customer attends or collects from
  the vendor's premises.

The applicable mode and any delivery charge are shown at or before checkout.

## 3. Service area and address

Delivery is available only in supported areas. The current application limits
calculated product delivery to supported locations in Abuja. Available areas
are displayed at checkout and may be expanded over time.

Customers must provide a complete, accurate, accessible delivery or pickup
address and a working telephone number. A customer is responsible for errors,
delay, or additional cost caused by an incorrect address or inability to
contact or access the recipient.

## 4. Fees and estimates

Delivery fees are calculated separately from the vendor's product or service
subtotal and may depend on distance and the number of required delivery legs.
Any time shown is an estimate, not a guarantee. Preparation time, traffic,
weather, vendor workload, item complexity, access restrictions, safety events,
and circumstances outside reasonable control may affect fulfilment.

The vendor must promptly disclose any material delay. Green Pack Delight may
help the parties agree a revised time, cancellation, replacement, or other fair
resolution.

## 5. Packaging, condition, and restricted items

Vendors must package goods appropriately for their nature and the expected
journey and must comply with food, health, safety, labelling, and other
applicable rules. Couriers may refuse an item that is illegal, prohibited,
unsafe, leaking, improperly packaged, materially different from the order, or
too large or heavy for the assigned transport.

The vendor remains responsible for the goods and their conformity before
handover. The courier is responsible for reasonable care from confirmed pickup
until confirmed delivery, subject to the nature and packaging of the item.

## 6. Pickup and delivery confirmation

Courier status updates may include assigned, picking up, at shop, returning,
delivered, or cancelled. The Platform's order, message, location, and delivery
records may be used to investigate a complaint. The customer should inspect
the package promptly and report visible loss, damage, tampering, or an incorrect
item as soon as reasonably possible with photographs where available.

## 7. Failed delivery

A delivery may fail where the address is wrong or inaccessible, the recipient
cannot be contacted, the customer is unavailable, the item is refused without
a valid reason, or delivery would be unsafe or unlawful. Green Pack Delight
will attempt to contact the relevant parties and may arrange redelivery or
return to the vendor. A reasonable additional delivery or return fee may apply
where the failure was caused by the customer, provided the fee is disclosed
before the new trip.

## 8. Loss, damage, and delay

The affected user should report an issue through support@greenpackdelight.com
with the order reference, description, photographs, and any other relevant
evidence. Green Pack Delight will review responsibility among the vendor,
courier, and customer and may arrange replacement, redelivery, re-performance,
partial refund, full refund, or another appropriate remedy under the Returns
and Refunds Policy and applicable law.

## 9. Contact

Shipping or fulfilment questions may be submitted through
www.greenpackdelight.com/help or support@greenpackdelight.com.
