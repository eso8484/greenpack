## 1. Vendor verification and KYC

Green Pack Delight is completing an automated, risk-based onboarding and
verification process.
Applicants provide their full name, date of birth, verified email address,
telephone number, business name, business category, physical operating
address, business contact details, and settlement bank account details.

Before a vendor is permitted to receive marketplace payments, we verify the
email address using a single-use OTP and resolve the settlement bank account
through Paystack so that the account-holder name can be checked against the
vendor or business information.

Vendor applicants will be required to provide either a National Identification
Number (NIN) or Bank Verification Number (BVN), together with applicable
business-registration evidence such as a Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
certificate. The NIN or BVN will be checked through an approved Nigerian
identity-verification provider, and CAC details will be checked against an
authoritative company-registration record or an approved business-verification
service. The applicant's identity and business details must be consistent with
the resolved settlement-account holder before payment capability is enabled.

KYC information is verified automatically where reliable source data is

dates of birth, addresses, document details, business ownership information, and bank
account-holder names are checked for consistency. Documents are checked for
legibility, validity, expiry, signs of alteration, and relevance to the
applicant. Higher-risk or inconsistent applications are rejected or escalated
for additional evidence and re-verification. Vendor access may be suspended
when information expires, changes materially, or cannot be reconfirmed.

We recognise that entering a NIN or document number is not, by itself,

where it has been checked against an authoritative source, an approved
verification provider, or the original document during a documented manual
review.

## 3. Receipt of payments on behalf of vendors

Customers initiate payment on Green Pack Delight's checkout, and Paystack
processes the transaction using a split-payment arrangement. Each
payment-enabled vendor has a Paystack subaccount. For the product or service
subtotal, 97% is allocated to the vendor subaccount and 3% is Green Pack
Delight's platform fee. The delivery fee is retained on Green Pack Delight's
main Paystack account solely so it can be paid to the courier who completes
the delivery.

Accordingly, Green Pack Delight facilitates the payment, but the vendor's
share is routed through Paystack's split settlement to the vendor's registered
subaccount rather than being maintained as an internal Green Pack balance.

## 4. Whether funds are held before disbursement

Green Pack Delight does not operate an escrow or deposit-taking service and
does not maintain stored-value balances for vendors. Vendor settlement is
handled under Paystack's subaccount settlement process and timetable.

The delivery-fee component is retained until delivery completion because it
is payable to the courier, not the vendor. Once the assigned courier marks the
delivery as completed, the platform initiates a Paystack transfer to the
courier's verified transfer-recipient bank account. Thus, the courier fee is
held only for the fulfilment period, from successful customer payment until
confirmed delivery. The duration varies with the order or service and is not a
fixed savings or investment period.

Identity and business verification is intended to be completed instantly where
the submitted information is accurate and the verification provider and source
registries are available. An exception is referred for manual review rather
than being approved automatically.

Payments may also be paused where a transaction is suspected to be fraudulent,
is disputed, has been cancelled, or requires investigation or refund.

## 5. Wallets or balances

No. Vendors, couriers, and customers do not have wallets or stored-value
balances on Green Pack Delight. They cannot transfer funds to another user's
or vendor's balance. Vendor settlement and courier transfers go to bank
accounts registered with Paystack.

## 6. Fulfilment model

Green Pack Delight is a marketplace and coordination platform, not a warehouse
or fulfilment centre. Products and service items remain with independent
third-party vendors. Depending on the order type, the vendor may provide the
service at its premises, travel to the customer, or hand the item to an
approved independent courier coordinated through the platform. For pickup and
return services, a courier may collect an item from the customer, take it to
the vendor, and return it after service completion. Green Pack Delight does
not hold inventory as a fulfilment centre.

## 7. Refunds, cancellations, and disputes

Customers may cancel an order through the platform while it is still pending.
After acceptance or fulfilment has started, cancellation is handled through
support because the vendor or courier may already have incurred costs.

For a complaint, the platform records the order and payment details and asks
the customer, vendor, and courier, where relevant, for supporting evidence such
as messages, photographs, delivery confirmation, or proof of service. We then
assess whether the appropriate outcome is cancellation, replacement,
re-performance, redelivery, full or partial refund, or rejection of the claim.
Fraud, safety, and chargeback matters may result in immediate account or payout
restrictions while investigated.

Approved refunds are submitted through Paystack within 1-2 business days and
returned to the original payment method;
they are not issued as a Green Pack wallet balance. Please see the attached
Cancellation, Returns and Refunds Policy for the applicable eligibility,
exceptions, and process.
