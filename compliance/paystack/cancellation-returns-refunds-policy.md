# Green Pack Delight Cancellation, Returns and Refunds Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

This policy applies to orders paid for through Green Pack Delight. It does not
remove any non-excludable right or remedy under Nigerian consumer protection
law.

## 1. Cancelling an order

A customer may cancel an order while its status is **pending**. Once a vendor
has accepted or started preparing an order, a courier has begun a trip, or a
service has started, cancellation must be requested through support and is not
automatically guaranteed.

Where a later cancellation is accepted, Green Pack Delight may deduct a
reasonable, evidenced amount for work already performed, perishable or custom
goods already prepared, and delivery activity already completed, to the extent
permitted by law and disclosed to the customer.

A vendor may cancel where an item is unavailable, the listing contained a
material error, fulfilment would be unsafe or unlawful, or the vendor cannot
perform the service. Repeated vendor cancellations may result in account
action. If the customer has paid and receives no substitute they accept, the
unfulfilled amount will be refunded.

## 2. Return or remedy eligibility

A customer may request a return, replacement, re-performance, price reduction,
or refund where, for example:

- the wrong item or materially wrong quantity was supplied;
- goods arrived damaged, materially defective, counterfeit, unsafe, expired,
  or materially different from the listing;
- a paid order was not delivered or a paid service was not provided;
- a service was materially below the description or agreed standard; or
- applicable law otherwise requires a remedy.

Change-of-mind returns are accepted only where the vendor's listing expressly
allows them and the item is unused, complete, and in resalable condition.

## 3. Non-returnable items

Unless defective, incorrectly supplied, or otherwise required by law, the
following are generally not returnable after delivery or performance:

- perishable food or goods with a short shelf life;
- personalised, altered, made-to-order, or custom-prepared items;
- opened hygiene-sensitive, cosmetic, health, or intimate products;
- goods damaged through misuse, improper handling, or unauthorised repair;
- services already fully performed with the customer's agreement; and
- digital or consumable items once supplied or activated.

## 4. Time for reporting

Customers should report visible delivery damage, tampering, missing items, or
wrong items within **24 hours** of delivery. Other defects or
service complaints should be reported within **7 days** or any
longer mandatory period under applicable law. A short reporting target will
not be used to defeat a statutory right relating to a latent defect that could
not reasonably have been discovered earlier.

## 5. How to make a claim

Contact support@greenpackdelight.com or use www.greenpackdelight.com/help and provide:

- the order reference and contact details;
- the item or service affected and the requested remedy;
- a clear description of the issue;
- photographs or video where relevant; and
- any messages, delivery information, receipt, or other useful evidence.

The customer must preserve the item and packaging where reasonably possible
and must not return an item without instructions. Green Pack Delight may
arrange courier collection, direct return to the vendor, inspection, or remote
evidence review.

## 6. Investigation and disputes

Green Pack Delight may obtain information from the customer, vendor, courier,
Paystack, and Platform records. Parties must cooperate and must not alter or
fabricate evidence. We aim to acknowledge a complaint promptly and communicate
an outcome within **7 days**. Serious or complex fraud, chargeback, safety, or
technical cases may require up to **10 business days** or longer where an
external bank, payment provider, regulator, or law-enforcement process controls
the timing. We will provide progress updates where a final decision cannot be
made within the target period.

Possible outcomes include no refund, full or partial refund, replacement,
redelivery, repair, re-performance, or account action. Our marketplace review
does not prevent a party from exercising rights available under law or through
Paystack or their payment provider.

## 7. Refund method and timing

Approved refunds are sent through Paystack to the original payment method.
Green Pack Delight does not issue refunds into a stored-value wallet. Once the
refund is submitted, the customer's bank or card issuer controls final posting
time. We will provide confirmation or a reference where available.

We submit an approved refund to Paystack within **1-2 business days**. The
customer's bank or card issuer may require additional time to post the credit.

Delivery fees are refundable where delivery was not performed or where the
refund results from vendor, courier, or Platform failure. They may be
non-refundable where the delivery was completed correctly and the return is a
permitted change of mind, subject to applicable law.

## 8. Chargebacks and fraud

A customer should contact support first where practical so the issue can be
resolved promptly. Green Pack Delight may provide transaction, fulfilment, and
communication records to Paystack or financial institutions in response to a
chargeback. Fraudulent claims, false evidence, or repeated abuse may result in
account restriction and reporting to the appropriate authorities.
